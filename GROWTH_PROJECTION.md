## Growth Drivers & Assumptions

### Primary Growth Factors

1. **Optimism Grant Funding**
   - Liquidity mining incentives (Months 1-6)
   - User acquisition campaigns
   - Partnership development budget
   - Estimated impact: 30-40% of Month 3 TVL

2. **Audit Completion**
   - Security validation builds user trust
   - Enables institutional/whale participation
   - Reduces smart contract risk perception

3. **Optimism Ecosystem Integration**
   - Native OP token support
   - Integration with Optimism DeFi tools
   - Cross-promotion with ecosystem projects
   - Superchain interoperability (future)

4. **Product Differentiation**
   - Native CLOB (better UX than AMM-based markets)
   - Oracle integration (Stork.network)
   - Cross-chain messaging (L2-to-L2)
   - ERC4626 LP vaults (standard DeFi interface)

5. **Market Timing**
   - 2025: Major sports events, elections, crypto volatility
   - Growing prediction market adoption
   - L2 scaling narrative

### Risk Factors & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Competing protocol launches | High | First-mover advantage, superior UX |
| Low initial liquidity | Medium | Grant-funded LP incentives |
| User acquisition costs | Medium | Ecosystem partnerships, organic growth |
| Market volatility (OP price) | Medium | TVL measured in OP, not USD |
| Regulatory uncertainty | Low | Decentralized, non-custodial design |
